## Introduction

Roswell became a UFO tourism destination by turning a disputed 1947 military incident into a lasting public memory landscape built around museums, festivals, themed businesses and civic identity. The town’s importance in [UFO culture]({{ 'ufo-culture/' | relative_url }}) comes less from the physical evidence of the original event than from how generations of visitors, researchers, entrepreneurs and residents transformed the story into a place-based experience connected to [science fiction]({{ 'science-fiction/' | relative_url }}), space exploration and ideas about hidden knowledge.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://roswell-nm.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_04202026-3027" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM Official Website]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Roswell NM Official Website Agenda</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NM Official WebsiteAgendaApril 16, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 16, 2026</span></span></span>

Visitors can explore the International UFO Museum and Research Center, attend the annual UFO Festival, encounter alien-themed public imagery and participate in a community identity built around the mystery of what happened in 1947. The result is a rare example of a modern legend becoming a tourism economy and a symbol recognised worldwide.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswellufomuseum.com/about-us" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswellufomuseum.com">[roswellufomuseum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswellufomuseum.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">About Us &#124; International UFO Musum &amp; Research Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">About Us &#124; International UFO Musum &amp; Research Center...</span></span></span>

## The rise of UFO tourism

### From local controversy to global destination

Roswell’s transformation into a UFO destination developed gradually rather than immediately after 1947. The original incident attracted attention because of conflicting military statements, but the story became a major part of popular culture decades later when UFO researchers, books, documentaries and television programmes revived interest in the case. By the late twentieth century, the event had become closely associated with wider UFO themes: government secrecy, recovered technology and the possibility of extraterrestrial contact.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://roswell-nm.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_04202026-3027" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM Official Website]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Roswell NM Official Website Agenda</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NM Official WebsiteAgendaApril 16, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 16, 2026</span></span></span>

Local tourism efforts then turned this cultural attention into a deliberate destination strategy. The city recognised that the UFO narrative could provide an identity distinct from many other small communities in the American Southwest. Rather than relying only on conventional heritage attractions, Roswell developed a visitor experience centred on curiosity and imagination. The city’s own historical materials describe UFO-related tourism as part of efforts by local officials and businesses to attract visitors interested in UFOs, science fiction and alien themes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/654/Our-History" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM Official Website]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Roswell NM Official Website Our History &#124; Roswell, NM</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NM Official Website Our History &#124; Roswell, NM</span></span></span>

The annual UFO Festival became a major part of this strategy. Established in 1995, the festival was created specifically to promote tourism and economic development by making Roswell a recognised destination connected to the UFO story.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/DocumentCenter/View/20308/UFO-2022-Report?bidId=" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM Official Website]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Roswell NM Official Website UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">FESTIVAL...</span></span></span> The event combines lectures, entertainment, themed activities, costumes and community celebrations, allowing both dedicated UFO enthusiasts and casual visitors to participate.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.newmexico.org/events/summer-events/roswell-ufo-festival/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newmexico.org">[New Mexico]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newmexico.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">New Mexico Roswell UFO Festival</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Mexico Roswell UFO Festival</span></span></span>

The festival also demonstrates how public memory can become an economic resource. The city reported that the 2022 UFO Festival generated an estimated $2.19 million in direct economic impact and attracted more than 40,000 visitors, while the 2023 festival generated a smaller but still significant direct impact of more than $510,000 with over 3,370 visitors. Differences between years reflect changing event formats and attendance patterns, but the figures show that UFO culture remains commercially important for Roswell.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/1757/UFO-Festival-Report-2022" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM Official Website]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Festival Report 2022</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Roswell NM Official WebsiteUFO Festival Report 2022 &#124; Roswell, NM...</span></span></span>

## Museums and public attractions

### The International UFO Museum as a memory institution

The centre of Roswell’s UFO identity is the International UFO Museum and Research Center. Established as a non-profit organisation in 1991 and opened to visitors in 1992, the museum was created to collect and preserve material connected with the Roswell Incident and wider UFO research.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.newmexico.org/places-to-visit/regions/southeast/roswell-ufo-museum/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newmexico.org">[New Mexico]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newmexico.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">New Mexico Roswell, NM International UFO Museum &amp; Research Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Mexico Roswell, NM International UFO Museum &amp; Research Center</span></span></span>

The museum plays a distinctive role because it does not simply display objects; it preserves a debate. Its exhibitions present accounts, documents and interpretations surrounding [UFO reports]({{ 'ufo-reports/' | relative_url }}), allowing visitors to encounter the competing narratives that have shaped Roswell’s reputation. The museum describes its purpose as informing the public and maintaining materials related to UFO phenomena rather than acting only as a traditional historical archive.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswellufomuseum.com/about-us" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswellufomuseum.com">[roswellufomuseum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswellufomuseum.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">About Us &#124; International UFO Musum &amp; Research Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">About Us &#124; International UFO Musum &amp; Research Center...</span></span></span>

This approach reflects the unusual nature of UFO tourism. A conventional historical attraction often depends on a settled narrative, while Roswell’s appeal depends on uncertainty. Visitors are drawn not only by a claim about what happened but also by the continuing disagreement over how the event should be understood. The attraction lies in the unresolved space between official explanations, personal testimony and cultural imagination.

## Mythmaking through local identity

### Turning a disputed event into community character

Roswell’s most significant achievement has been converting a controversial story into a shared civic identity. The town does not need universal agreement about the 1947 event for the story to function culturally. Believers, sceptics and curious tourists can all participate in the same public experience because the attraction is based on exploration, mystery and storytelling as much as on a specific conclusion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.newmexico.org/places-to-visit/regions/southeast/roswell-ufo-museum/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newmexico.org">[New Mexico]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newmexico.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">New Mexico Roswell, NM International UFO Museum &amp; Research Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Mexico Roswell, NM International UFO Museum &amp; Research Center</span></span></span>
