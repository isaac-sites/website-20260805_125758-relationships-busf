## From science fiction art to emoji

The flying saucer emoji did not create the UFO symbol; it inherited a design language built through twentieth-century science fiction. The circular craft with a domed top, often shown hovering with lights or a beam beneath it, became one of the clearest visual markers of [alien technology]({{ 'alien-technology/' | relative_url }}). Films, television, book covers and toys repeatedly reinforced the association between the saucer shape and imagined extraterrestrial contact.

Emoji adoption changed the role of this image. In earlier media, a UFO illustration usually belonged to a fictional scene or a deliberate reference to science fiction. In digital conversation, the same symbol became available as a single character that could be inserted instantly into a message. The flying saucer moved from being a representation of an object in a story to being a communication tool.

The technical step behind this shift was standardisation. Emoji became widely interoperable after being incorporated into Unicode, the international character system that allows text and symbols to appear consistently across different devices and platforms. Unicode’s emoji framework expanded from early mobile pictographs into a large cross-platform collection, allowing cultural symbols to function like shared digital characters.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.unicode.org/standard/reports/tr51/tr51-7.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unicode.org">[Unicode]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unicode.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UTR #51: Unicode Emoji</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UTR #51: Unicode Emoji...</span></span></span>

The flying saucer emoji, represented as 🛸, was approved as part of Unicode 10.0 and Emoji 5.0 in 2017. Its inclusion placed [UFO imagery]({{ 'ufo-imagery/' | relative_url }}) alongside other globally recognised pictographs, turning a science-fiction motif into a standard element of modern digital expression.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://emojipedia.org/flying-saucer" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: emojipedia.org">[Emojipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">emojipedia.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">🛸 Flying Saucer Emoji &#124; Meaning, Copy And Paste</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Emojipedia🛸 Flying Saucer Emoji &#124; Meaning, Copy And Paste...</span></span></span>

## Digital shorthand and shared meanings

The meaning of the UFO emoji depends less on a single definition than on the cultural history behind the symbol. A user adding 🛸 to a message may be referring to aliens, space, unusual experiences, futuristic technology or something that feels bizarre and unexpected. Like many emoji, its meaning comes from context rather than from a fixed dictionary definition. Research on emoji interpretation shows that these symbols often carry multiple possible meanings depending on surrounding words and social situations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.06302" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">arXiv On the Context-Free Ambiguity of Emoji</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">On the Context-Free Ambiguity of EmojiJanuary 17, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 17, 2022</span></span></span>

One reason the UFO emoji communicates so quickly is that science fiction created a ready-made visual code before digital platforms existed. A rocket may suggest human exploration, while a flying saucer suggests something unfamiliar or outside normal experience. This distinction allows the emoji to function as a shorthand for ideas such as mystery, curiosity, imagination and surprise.

The emoji also demonstrates how digital communication preserves older cultural symbols while changing their purpose. A person using 🛸 does not necessarily refer to UFO investigations or a belief in extraterrestrial visitors. The symbol may instead be used jokingly to describe an unusual idea, an unexpected event or a feeling of being disconnected from ordinary life. The same image that once represented dramatic alien encounters can now work as casual online expression.

This flexibility mirrors the broader development of emoji as a visual language. ## UFO symbols in modern communication

The mainstreaming of the flying saucer emoji shows how science-fiction imagery can become detached from its original fictional setting while remaining culturally recognisable. A symbol that once required explanation in a film poster or magazine illustration can now appear in a conversation between people who may never have followed UFO stories closely.

Its adoption also reflects the broader relationship between science fiction and everyday technology. Science-fiction ideas frequently move through a cycle: first imagined in fiction, then represented in popular culture, and eventually absorbed into ordinary design and communication. The UFO emoji represents one stage of that process, where an imagined spacecraft becomes a routine part of digital interaction.

The choice to include a flying saucer in the emoji catalogue was also a cultural decision about representation. Emoji collections are not neutral lists of objects; they are designed around symbols that communities recognise and use. The presence of 🛸 indicates that UFO imagery had become sufficiently widespread that a global digital standard considered it a meaningful part of contemporary visual communication.     if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
