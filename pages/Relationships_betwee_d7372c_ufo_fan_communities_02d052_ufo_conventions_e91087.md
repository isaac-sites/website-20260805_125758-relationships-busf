## Introduction

UFO conventions are more than meetings about unexplained sightings. They are organised social spaces where people interested in unidentified phenomena, extraterrestrial possibilities, [science fiction]({{ 'science-fiction/' | relative_url }}), and alternative ideas about humanity’s place in the universe create shared experiences and identities. Through lectures, exhibitions, costumes, storytelling sessions and informal conversations, conventions transform a private interest into a visible community. Events such as the Roswell UFO Festival, the International UFO Congress and Contact in the Desert show how [UFO culture]({{ 'ufo-culture/' | relative_url }}) combines investigation, entertainment and belonging in the same physical spaces.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=19049&amp;calType=0&amp;day=7&amp;month=7&amp;year=2026" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[roswell-nm.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Roswell NMCalendar • Roswell UFO Festival</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 2, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2026</span></span></span>

These gatherings do not require every participant to share the same interpretation of UFOs. Some attendees approach the subject as a research question, some as science fiction-inspired curiosity, and others as a cultural interest or personal hobby. The common identity is often built around participation itself: attending talks, collecting materials, meeting familiar figures and recognising shared symbols. In this way, UFO conventions reveal how an unusual interest can become a structured community with its own rituals, spaces and forms of expression.

## Conference spaces and community rituals

### How gatherings turn interest into belonging

UFO conventions create community by giving enthusiasts a place where a subject often treated as unusual in everyday life becomes normalised. A person who rarely discusses UFOs with colleagues or family can enter a convention environment where conversations about [sightings]({{ 'sightings/' | relative_url }}), space exploration, government investigations, science fiction and [extraterrestrial life]({{ 'extraterrestrial-life/' | relative_url }}) are expected parts of social interaction.

The structure of these events encourages repeated forms of participation. Attendees listen to speakers, visit exhibition areas, exchange stories and return to familiar annual gatherings. Over time, these repeated activities can create a sense of membership even among people who have never met before. The convention becomes not just a place to receive information but a place to recognise others who share an interest.

Large UFO conferences demonstrate this pattern. The International UFO Congress, founded in 1991, has developed into an annual event featuring speakers, exhibitors and discussions covering subjects such as [UFO reports]({{ 'ufo-reports/' | relative_url }}), technology, government investigations and possible extraterrestrial explanations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufocongress.com/ufo-conference/about/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufocongress.com">[UFO Congress]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufocongress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufocongress.com.</span></span></span> Contact in the Desert similarly combines lectures, workshops and intensive discussion sessions, creating multiple levels of participation from casual visitors to dedicated attendees.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://contactinthedesert.com/about/faq/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: contactinthedesert.com">[contactinthedesert.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">contactinthedesert.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">FAQ - Contact in the Desert &#124; The Largest UFO Conference in the World...</span></span></span>

The social experience is important because UFO culture has historically existed between categories. It can involve scientific curiosity, popular entertainment, personal experience and speculative storytelling at the same time. Conventions provide a setting where these different approaches can coexist, even when participants disagree about what UFO phenomena represent.

### Roswell as a shared cultural destination

The annual Roswell UFO Festival is one of the clearest examples of a UFO gathering becoming a community ritual. The event developed around the 1947 Roswell incident, a disputed episode that became one of the most recognisable symbols of UFO culture. Rather than functioning only as a conference, the festival combines public celebration, historical discussion, entertainment and themed activities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswellufomuseum.com/about-us" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswellufomuseum.com">[roswellufomuseum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswellufomuseum.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">About Us &#124; International UFO Musum &amp; Research Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">About Us &#124; International UFO Musum &amp; Research Center...</span></span></span>

Roswell shows how a location can become meaningful through repeated community activity. Visitors are not only consuming information about the incident; they are participating in a place associated with a shared story. The town’s UFO identity is reinforced through museums, annual events and public performances. The International UFO Museum & Research Center preserves materials connected with the Roswell incident and other UFO-related topics, helping maintain the location as a meeting point for enthusiasts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswellufomuseum.com/hours-of-operation" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswellufomuseum.com">[roswellufomuseum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswellufomuseum.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Hours of Operation &#124; International UFO Museum &amp; Research Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Hours of Operation &#124; International UFO Museum &amp; Research Center...</span></span></span>

The festival atmosphere also broadens participation beyond dedicated researchers. Official festival activities have included costume contests, entertainment, vendors and family-oriented events, allowing people to engage with UFO culture through humour and creativity as well as investigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=19121&amp;calType=0&amp;day=1&amp;month=7&amp;year=2026" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Roswell NMCalendar • UFO Festival Alien Pet Costume Contest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 4, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 4, 2026</span></span></span> This mixture helps explain why UFO conventions often resemble fan conventions as much as academic conferences.

## Lectures, costumes and cultural performances

### Knowledge sharing as a social activity

Lectures are central to UFO conventions, but their role is not limited to presenting information. They also create moments of collective attention. A speaker discussing UFO cases, space science or possible explanations for unusual events provides a shared experience for an audience that already has an interest in the topic.

At Contact in the Desert, for example, the programme includes lectures and workshops covering UAP (unidentified anomalous phenomena), space, technology, consciousness and reports of encounters.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://contactinthedesert.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: contactinthedesert.com">[contactinthedesert.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">contactinthedesert.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Contact in the Desert: Infinite Realms &#124; May 28</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">June 1, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1, 2026</span></span></span> The range of subjects reflects the broad cultural territory occupied by modern UFO gatherings, where scientific questions, personal narratives and speculative ideas often appear alongside one another.

This creates a distinctive convention environment. Participants are not simply fans watching a performance; many see themselves as active contributors to an ongoing conversation. They may ask questions, share experiences, collect research materials or debate interpretations with other attendees.

### Costumes and symbols make identity visible

Costumes and alien imagery play a different but equally important role. They allow attendees to display membership in a community through visual symbols. The classic image of the alien, flying saucer or astronaut becomes a shared language that can communicate enthusiasm without requiring a conversation.

Roswell’s festival demonstrates this performative side of UFO culture. Alien-themed costume events and parades encourage participants to publicly adopt the imagery associated with the community.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roswell-nm.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=19120" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roswell-nm.gov">[Roswell NM]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roswell-nm.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Roswell NMCalendar • UFO Festival Alien Costume Contest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 3, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 3, 2026</span></span></span> These activities are often playful rather than expressions of literal belief. A person dressed as an alien may be celebrating science fiction aesthetics, participating in local culture or simply enjoying the humour attached to UFO traditions.

This flexibility is one reason UFO conventions overlap strongly with science fiction fandom. Both communities use costumes, fictional imagery and imaginative worlds to create shared experiences. However, UFO conventions add another layer: many participants are also engaging with real-world questions, personal accounts and claims about unexplained events.

### The balance between serious investigation and entertainment

A defining feature of UFO gatherings is the coexistence of serious and playful elements. A conference hall may host discussions about government documents or scientific possibilities while the same event includes merchandise stalls, themed artwork and entertainment.

This mixture sometimes creates tension. Critics argue that entertainment can blur the difference between evidence-based investigation and speculation. Supporters argue that cultural activities are part of how communities communicate, attract newcomers and preserve interest in a subject.

The result is a hybrid form of gathering. UFO conventions are neither purely scientific meetings nor simply fan festivals. They operate as cultural spaces where knowledge, identity and performance overlap.

## Belonging among UFO enthusiasts

### Building identity around a shared mystery

For many attendees, the strongest appeal of UFO conventions is the opportunity to belong to a group organised around curiosity. The subject provides a common point of connection for people who may have different backgrounds, beliefs and reasons for attending.

Shared identity does not necessarily mean complete agreement. UFO communities often contain disagreements over explanations, evidence standards and the meaning of reported experiences. Some participants focus on investigation and documentation, while others are interested in spirituality, science fiction or cultural history. Conventions provide a space where these different interests can interact.

This diversity is important because UFO identity is often built around a relationship with uncertainty. The central theme is not always a fixed answer but the idea that unanswered questions deserve attention. That attitude creates a community based on exploration rather than a single established doctrine.

### Why conventions matter within UFO culture


<div class="content-callout content-callout--key content-callout--takeaway" markdown="1">

UFO conventions help maintain the wider UFO community by turning an abstract interest into a social network. Books, films and online discussions can introduce people to UFO ideas, but physical gatherings provide direct relationships and shared memories.

</div>

They also preserve connections between UFO culture and science fiction. The same imagination that shaped stories about alien worlds appears in convention art, costumes and discussions about humanity’s future. At the same time, UFO gatherings maintain a separate identity by focusing on reported encounters, investigations and the possibility that some mysteries may exist beyond current understanding.

The importance of these events is therefore cultural rather than simply evidential. Whether attendees view UFOs as scientific mysteries, folklore, entertainment or a mixture of all three, conventions show how communities form around questions about the unknown. They turn individual curiosity into collective identity through repeated rituals, shared symbols and experiences that connect strangers with a common interest.

    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
