## Introduction

[UFO documentary]({{ 'documentary-debates/' | relative_url }}) editing does not create mystery by adding fiction to a factual subject; it creates mystery by deciding how real-world material is arranged. Interviews, archive clips, military documents, witness statements, maps, silence, music and unanswered questions are combined into a narrative that guides viewers through uncertainty. The central editing mechanism is not simply showing unusual events, but controlling when information appears, whose perspective is prioritised and how different pieces of evidence are made to relate to one another.

This matters because [UFO documentaries]({{ 'ufo-documentaries/' | relative_url }}) often operate at the boundary between investigation and [science-fiction]({{ 'science-fiction/' | relative_url }}) storytelling. They borrow familiar science-fiction structures — discovery, hidden knowledge, unknown technology and possible contact — while using documentary techniques that imply factual examination. Films such as *The Phenomenon* use government interviews, historical footage and witness testimony to build a chronological investigation of UFO reports, demonstrating how editing can transform scattered events into a coherent public narrative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_phenomenon" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rottentomatoes.com">[Rotten Tomatoes]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rottentomatoes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rotten Tomatoes The Phenomenon &#124; Rotten Tomatoes</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Rotten TomatoesThe Phenomenon &#124; Rotten TomatoesOctober 6, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 6, 2020</span></span></span>

The editing choices do not by themselves prove or disprove UFO claims. Instead, they shape the experience of uncertainty: whether viewers feel they are watching a careful investigation, an unresolved puzzle, a personal testimony collection or a dramatic mystery.

## Building suspense from real material

UFO documentaries construct suspense differently from fictional science-fiction films because they usually cannot rely on invented scenes or predetermined endings. Editors work with incomplete material: interviews recorded years after events, damaged archive footage, conflicting accounts and documents with uncertain significance. The mystery emerges from the gaps between these pieces.

A common structure begins with a compelling event, introduces witnesses, expands the circle of participants and gradually adds official or historical context. The viewer is encouraged to hold several possibilities in mind before a final interpretation is offered — or deliberately withheld. This resembles investigative storytelling, where the order of disclosure becomes part of the argument.

For example, *The Phenomenon* organises decades of [UFO reports]({{ 'ufo-reports/' | relative_url }}) into a historical progression, moving between archival interviews, modern commentary and accounts involving military personnel and government officials. Its effectiveness depends heavily on sequencing: older cases establish a long-running pattern, while later material involving Pentagon UFO videos gives the story contemporary relevance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_phenomenon" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rottentomatoes.com">[Rotten Tomatoes]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rottentomatoes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rotten Tomatoes The Phenomenon &#124; Rotten Tomatoes</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Rotten TomatoesThe Phenomenon &#124; Rotten TomatoesOctober 6, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 6, 2020</span></span></span>

### The power of delayed answers

One of the strongest editing tools in mystery documentaries is delayed explanation. Editors may introduce a witness before revealing all available background information, or show an unusual piece of footage before explaining its limitations. This creates a temporary information imbalance between filmmaker and audience.

The technique resembles suspense methods used in fiction, where uncertainty encourages attention. In documentary form, however, it carries an additional responsibility: viewers may interpret the order of presentation as evidence that the filmmaker has discovered a meaningful connection.

This is where editing can influence perception without altering individual facts. A statement from one witness placed directly before an official response may feel like a confrontation; the same statements separated by historical explanation may feel like a broader debate.

Film theory has long examined this effect. The Kuleshov effect describes how audiences create meaning from the relationship between shots rather than from isolated images alone. The principle demonstrates why the placement of archive footage, interview reactions and contextual material can change interpretation even when the original material remains unchanged.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://facingthemind.leeds.ac.uk/face-as-window/the-kuleshov-effect/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: facingthemind.leeds.ac.uk">[Facing the Mind]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">facingthemind.leeds.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Facing the Mind The Kuleshov Effect &#124; Facing the Mind</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Facing the MindThe Kuleshov Effect &#124; Facing the Mind - Film/Art/Mental Health...</span></span></span>

## Interviews, archives and narrative order

Interviews are the foundation of many UFO documentaries because they provide human experience as the emotional centre of the mystery. Editing determines whether a witness appears as a reliable observer, a personal storyteller or part of a larger pattern.

A typical UFO documentary may combine several interview categories:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--comparison" markdown="1">

* **Eyewitnesses** provide personal accounts and emotional detail.
* **Military or government figures** add institutional authority.
* **Researchers and historians** provide interpretation.
* **Sceptics or alternative explanations** may introduce uncertainty or challenge conclusions.

</div>

The order of these voices strongly affects audience response. Beginning with a witness describing a dramatic event creates a different impression from beginning with a scientist explaining limitations in the evidence. Neither approach is automatically more accurate, but each creates a different narrative experience.

### Archive footage as a credibility signal

Archive material plays a particularly important role in UFO documentary editing because old photographs, news broadcasts, government clips and declassified documents visually suggest historical authenticity. Even when the meaning of an archive item remains disputed, its appearance within a carefully structured sequence can make it feel like part of an unfolding investigation.

This is especially visible in documentaries that revisit famous cases. Historical footage can connect past and present, allowing editors to create a sense of continuity between earlier UFO reports and modern discussions of unidentified anomalous phenomena. Films such as *The Phenomenon* use archival interviews and historical cases alongside contemporary officials and footage to create this bridge.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_phenomenon" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rottentomatoes.com">[Rotten Tomatoes]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rottentomatoes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rotten Tomatoes The Phenomenon &#124; Rotten Tomatoes</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Rotten TomatoesThe Phenomenon &#124; Rotten TomatoesOctober 6, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 6, 2020</span></span></span>

However, archive footage does not automatically resolve uncertainty. A document proving that a sighting was reported is not the same as a document proving what caused it. Editing can preserve that distinction, or it can blur it if different types of evidence are presented with the same dramatic weight.

## How presentation affects belief

The most important feature of UFO documentary editing is not whether it persuades viewers toward belief or disbelief, but how it frames the process of interpretation. A viewer rarely encounters a UFO claim as a single fact; they encounter a carefully arranged sequence of voices, images and explanations.

A documentary that emphasises official statements, technical analysis and competing explanations creates a different relationship with uncertainty than one that focuses mainly on personal testimony and unresolved questions.

### Music, pacing and atmosphere

Sound design and pacing also contribute to mystery construction. Slow reveals, pauses before important statements, dramatic music and repeated visual motifs can increase emotional intensity. These techniques are common in documentary storytelling generally, but they become especially significant in subjects where the evidence itself is incomplete.

The use of atmosphere can make a viewer feel that an unanswered question is important. That feeling may encourage curiosity and further investigation, but it can also make uncertainty feel like confirmation if emotional presentation is mistaken for evidence.

This distinction has become a major discussion point around modern UFO documentaries. ### Mystery construction versus manipulation

Editing is unavoidable in documentary filmmaking. Every documentary selects some material and excludes other material. The key issue is transparency: whether the structure helps audiences understand complexity or pushes them towards a predetermined conclusion.

A documentary can responsibly create suspense by showing what is known, what remains uncertain and why disagreement exists. Problems arise when editing removes important context, presents speculation alongside established facts without distinction, or uses cinematic techniques to imply certainty.

The documentary *Mirage Men* provides an interesting contrast because its mystery structure focuses not only on alleged UFO events but on how UFO stories themselves are produced, including claims about government influence and myth-making. Its approach demonstrates that the construction of a mystery can itself become the subject of investigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://filmsandfestivals.britishcouncil.org/projects/mirage-men" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: filmsandfestivals.britishcouncil.org">[British Council UK Films Database]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">filmsandfestivals.britishcouncil.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">British Council UK Films DatabaseBritish Council UK Films Database: Mirage MenMay 12, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 12, 2025</span></span></span>

## The role of editing in the UFO–science fiction relationship

UFO documentaries connect strongly with science fiction because their editing methods often reproduce science-fiction themes without openly becoming fictional. The unknown object becomes a narrative device; the investigation becomes a journey; missing information becomes a dramatic force.

This does not mean UFO documentaries are simply fictional stories. Rather, they use some of the same storytelling mechanisms that make science fiction compelling: anticipation, discovery, hidden possibilities and questions about humanity’s place in a larger universe.

The result is a distinctive form of public storytelling. Through editing, a collection of reports, interviews and historical fragments can become a mystery narrative that influences how audiences imagine unexplained phenomena. The central creative act is not inventing the unknown, but organising uncertainty into a story.

    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
