## Introduction

Fifth-kind encounters are claims that humans can communicate directly with non-human intelligence rather than merely observe an unexplained object or experience a close encounter. In [UFO culture]({{ 'ufo-culture/' | relative_url }}), the idea is usually associated with “Close Encounters of the Fifth Kind” (CE5), a term popularised by Steven Greer in the 1990s to describe attempts at human-initiated contact using methods such as group meditation, focused intention and observation of unusual aerial phenomena. Supporters view these practices as a possible pathway to peaceful interaction; critics argue that the claims rely heavily on personal experience, ambiguous observations and interpretations that are difficult to test scientifically.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drstevengreer.com/ce5-the-new-frontier-of-contact-2026/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drstevengreer.com">[Dr. Steven Greer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drstevengreer.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ce5 the new frontier of contact 2026</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dr. Steven GreerCE5: The New Frontier of Contact (2026) - Dr. Steven Greer...</span></span></span>

The fifth-kind debate is distinctive because it shifts the central question from “What was seen?” to “Can communication occur, and how would we recognise it?” This places UFO contact claims alongside a broader scientific and cultural problem: how an unknown intelligence might send, receive or interpret messages. [Science fiction]({{ 'science-fiction/' | relative_url }}) has explored this problem extensively, often treating communication itself as the hardest part of [first contact]({{ 'first-contact/' | relative_url }}) rather than the arrival of an alien visitor.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.simonandschuster.net/books/Contact/Carl-Sagan/9781501197987" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: simonandschuster.net">[Simon &amp; Schuster]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">simonandschuster.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Simon &amp; SchusterContact &#124; Book by Carl Sagan &#124; Official Publisher Page &#124; Simon &amp; SchusterFebruary 26, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 26, 2019</span></span></span>

## Different meanings of fifth kind

### From encounter reports to claimed dialogue

Earlier [UFO encounter categories]({{ 'ufo-encounter-categories/' | relative_url }}) mainly describe what witnesses report experiencing: seeing objects, observing effects or encountering entities. Fifth-kind claims introduce an active role for humans. Instead of waiting for an unknown intelligence to appear, participants attempt to initiate contact through deliberate actions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drstevengreer.com/ce5-the-new-frontier-of-contact-2026/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drstevengreer.com">[Dr. Steven Greer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drstevengreer.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ce5 the new frontier of contact 2026</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dr. Steven GreerCE5: The New Frontier of Contact (2026) - Dr. Steven Greer...</span></span></span>

CE5 groups commonly describe a process involving preparation, meditation, visualisation, collective attention and attempts to signal peaceful intent. Some participants report seeing lights or objects after these sessions and interpret them as responses. Within these communities, the mechanism is often described not simply as technological communication but as an interaction involving consciousness or non-verbal forms of awareness.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drstevengreer.com/ce5-the-new-frontier-of-contact-2026/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drstevengreer.com">[Dr. Steven Greer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drstevengreer.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ce5 the new frontier of contact 2026</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dr. Steven GreerCE5: The New Frontier of Contact (2026) - Dr. Steven Greer...</span></span></span>

The scientific challenge is that such claims involve several uncertain stages:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Observation:** Was an unusual light or object actually present?
* **Identification:** Was it an artificial craft, natural phenomenon, aircraft, satellite or another explanation?
* **Connection:** Is there evidence that the event occurred because of the contact attempt?
* **Communication:** Was information exchanged rather than simply an experience interpreted as meaningful?

</div>

These questions make fifth-kind claims difficult to evaluate using conventional scientific methods. A report of a personal experience may be meaningful to the witness while still lacking the measurable evidence needed to establish an external cause.

### Why communication claims are harder to verify

Communication requires more than detecting something unusual. Researchers studying possible extraterrestrial intelligence face the problem of distinguishing a genuine message from coincidence, natural phenomena or human-generated signals. SETI researchers have developed protocols for verifying possible signals and considering how humanity should respond after a confirmed detection.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seti.org/research/seti-101/protocols-for-an-eti-signal-detection/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seti.org">[SETI Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seti.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Institute Protocols for an ETI Signal Detection</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SETI InstituteProtocols for an ETI Signal DetectionJune 1, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1, 2026</span></span></span>

The same problem appears in fifth-kind discussions. A claimed message must contain information that is independently identifiable, repeatable and unlikely to have arisen from expectation or interpretation. Without such evidence, disagreements often focus less on the experience itself and more on how confidently conclusions are drawn from it.

## Communication in UFO culture

### The CE5 movement and the role of intention

Fifth-kind ideas became widely associated with Steven Greer and the Center for the Study of Extraterrestrial Intelligence (CSETI). Greer’s approach argues that peaceful human intention can facilitate contact and that humanity should approach potential non-human intelligence cooperatively rather than defensively.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drstevengreer.com/ce5-the-new-frontier-of-contact-2026/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drstevengreer.com">[Dr. Steven Greer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drstevengreer.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ce5 the new frontier of contact 2026</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dr. Steven GreerCE5: The New Frontier of Contact (2026) - Dr. Steven Greer...</span></span></span>

Supporters argue that conventional ideas of communication may be too limited. If another intelligence were far more advanced than humans, they suggest, it might not communicate through familiar radio signals or physical appearances. Instead, contact might involve methods humans do not yet understand.

Critics respond that expanding the definition of communication can create a problem of testability. If almost any unusual perception, coincidence or internal experience can be interpreted as a message, it becomes difficult to establish what evidence would count against the claim. This is a recurring issue in debates about extraordinary communication claims: a flexible interpretation may allow many experiences to feel significant, but it may also reduce the ability to distinguish genuine external signals from human meaning-making.

### The difference between contact claims and scientific communication searches

Fifth-kind claims are sometimes discussed alongside SETI, but the two approaches differ significantly.

SETI (Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence) searches for possible technological signals from elsewhere in the universe, usually through astronomical observations. It treats communication as a measurable problem involving signals, detection methods and verification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seti.org/research/seti-101/protocols-for-an-eti-signal-detection/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seti.org">[SETI Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seti.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Institute Protocols for an ETI Signal Detection</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SETI InstituteProtocols for an ETI Signal DetectionJune 1, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1, 2026</span></span></span>

CE5-style claims generally begin from the assumption that contact may already be possible through direct human interaction or consciousness-based methods. They focus on personal or group experiences rather than astronomical detection.

## Science fiction approaches to contact

Science fiction has often treated communication as the central challenge of alien encounters. Rather than focusing only on spacecraft or conflict, many stories ask how two radically different intelligences could understand each other.

### *Contact*: messages built from shared knowledge

Carl Sagan’s novel *Contact* presents first contact as a problem of decoding meaning. Humanity receives an artificial signal and must determine whether it contains a genuine message. The story explores the idea that mathematics and physics might provide common ground between civilisations with no shared language or culture.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.simonandschuster.net/books/Contact/Carl-Sagan/9781501197987" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: simonandschuster.net">[Simon &amp; Schuster]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">simonandschuster.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Simon &amp; SchusterContact &#124; Book by Carl Sagan &#124; Official Publisher Page &#124; Simon &amp; SchusterFebruary 26, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 26, 2019</span></span></span>

This approach closely resembles real discussions in SETI and Messaging to Extraterrestrial Intelligence (METI). Researchers have considered how a message could be designed so that an unknown civilisation might recognise patterns, structures or universal concepts rather than relying on human languages.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.4968" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">arXiv A Protocol for Messaging to Extraterrestrial Intelligence</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">A Protocol for Messaging to Extraterrestrial IntelligenceJanuary 25, 2011...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 25, 2011</span></span></span>

### *Arrival*: the problem of alien language

The film *Arrival*, based on Ted Chiang’s story “Story of Your Life”, explores a different communication problem: even if aliens are present, humans may not share the assumptions needed to interpret their language. The story places a linguist at the centre of first contact and examines how meaning, perception and misunderstanding shape communication.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.wired.com/story/arrival-science-fact-fiction" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wired.com">[WIRED]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wired.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The protagonist, linguist Louise Banks, works to understand the heptapods&#x27; strange circular written language and whale-like spoken sounds...</span></span></span>

### Why fiction matters to the fifth-kind debate

Science fiction provides a testing ground for ideas about contact because it can explore possibilities before evidence exists. Stories imagine communication through radio signals, mathematics, language, consciousness and shared experiences. These scenarios influence public expectations about what “contact” might look like.

At the same time, fiction can blur into belief. Some UFO communities use science-fiction concepts as frameworks for interpreting real experiences, while critics argue that familiar narratives can shape how ambiguous events are understood.

## The central debate: experience, evidence and interpretation

Fifth-kind contact claims remain controversial because they sit between personal experience and scientific investigation. A person may sincerely report a profound encounter, but sincerity alone does not establish the external source of the experience.

The strongest scientific case for communication would require evidence such as:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* independently verified observations;
* repeatable results under controlled conditions;
* information that could not reasonably come from known human sources;
* a clear exchange of information rather than an interpretation applied afterwards.

</div>

Until such evidence exists, fifth-kind encounters remain primarily a cultural and experiential phenomenon rather than an established method of communication with non-human intelligence.

Their importance within UFO culture and science fiction lies in the questions they raise: what counts as a message, how humans recognise intelligence, and whether communication with an unfamiliar mind is limited more by technology or by our ability to understand something genuinely different from ourselves.

    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
