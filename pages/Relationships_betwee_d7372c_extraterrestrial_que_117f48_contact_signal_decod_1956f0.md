## Introduction

Carl Sagan’s *Contact* imagines [first contact]({{ 'first-contact/' | relative_url }}) not as an alien spacecraft arriving on Earth, but as a scientific discovery: humanity detects an artificial signal, proves it is not a natural phenomenon, and struggles to understand what an unknown intelligence is trying to communicate. The story’s central idea is that the hardest part of meeting an extraterrestrial civilisation may not be travelling across space, but recognising and interpreting a message from a mind separated from us by distance, time and biology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seti.org/news/contact-movie/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seti.org">[SETI Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seti.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Institute Contact (Movie</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SETI InstituteContact (Movie)August 22, 2011...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 22, 2011</span></span></span>

The signal in *Contact* is built around a realistic SETI (Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence) problem. ## The artificial signal discovery

### Why *Contact* begins with listening rather than meeting

In Carl Sagan’s novel and its 1997 film adaptation, astronomer Ellie Arroway discovers a repeating transmission from the direction of Vega. The first clue is not a spoken message but a mathematical pattern: a sequence of prime numbers that appears too ordered to be produced by nature. The discovery transforms a routine search for signals into evidence that humanity may not be alone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0118884/faq/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: imdb.com">[IMDb]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">imdb.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Contact (1997) - FAQ - IMDb...</span></span></span>

This choice reflects one of the most important ideas in realistic alien-contact fiction: an advanced civilisation may first reveal itself through technology rather than biology. A distant civilisation could be impossible to observe directly, but a deliberately produced signal might travel across interstellar distances and leave a measurable trace.

Sagan’s approach was closely connected to real SETI thinking. Radio astronomers do not search simply for “alien sounds”; they look for unusual patterns, especially signals that appear narrow, structured or clearly associated with a specific astronomical source. A genuine detection would require repeated observation and independent confirmation because many unusual signals can have ordinary explanations, including human-made radio interference.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seti.org/about/faq" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seti.org">[SETI Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seti.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Institute FAQ</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SETI InstituteFAQ...</span></span></span>

### The problem of proving a signal is intelligent

The first challenge after detecting a strange transmission would be deciding whether it is actually artificial. This is where *Contact* captures a major scientific difficulty: intelligence is not identified by appearance alone but by patterns that are unlikely to occur naturally.

Modern SETI researchers face the same problem. Candidate signals must be examined for characteristics such as their direction of origin, frequency behaviour, repetition and whether they can be explained by Earth-based technology. The famous Proxima Centauri candidate signal known as BLC1, for example, initially attracted attention but was later determined to be linked to terrestrial interference rather than an extraterrestrial transmitter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seti.org/what-was-signal-proxima-centauri" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seti.org">[SETI Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seti.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Institute What was that signal from Proxima Centauri?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SETI InstituteWhat was that signal from Proxima Centauri?...</span></span></span>

The fictional discovery in *Contact* therefore works because it treats confirmation as a process rather than a single dramatic moment. The scientific community must move from suspicion to evidence before accepting that humanity has received a message from another civilisation.

## Decoding an unknown message

### From numbers to meaning

The most memorable part of *Contact* is not simply receiving the signal but discovering that it contains layers of information. After identifying mathematical patterns, scientists uncover a far more complex transmission containing instructions and designs. The story explores a fundamental communication problem: how can two civilisations that share no language, culture or history create a common starting point?

Mathematics becomes the bridge because it describes relationships rather than human-specific experiences. Numbers, physical constants and scientific concepts may provide a possible foundation for communication because they are based on features of the universe rather than a particular society. However, even mathematics is not automatically simple to translate. A message may still depend on assumptions about how information should be organised, visualised or interpreted.

The real Arecibo Message, transmitted in 1974, demonstrates both the ambition and limitations of this approach. Designed by Frank Drake, with contributions from Carl Sagan and others, it encoded information about humanity, DNA-related chemistry, Earth and the Solar System into 1,679 binary digits sent towards the globular cluster M13. It was primarily a demonstration of communication possibilities rather than an expectation of receiving a reply.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0265964611000415" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sciencedirect.com">[ScienceDirect]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sciencedirect.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ScienceDirect A protocol for messaging to extraterrestrial intelligence</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">A protocol for messaging to extraterrestrial intelligence - ScienceDirect...</span></span></span>

### The danger of assuming an alien mind works like ours

*Contact* also raises a deeper issue: decoding is not just a technical exercise. A message may be successfully converted into symbols while still being misunderstood.

Human languages contain cultural assumptions, shared experiences and historical references. An alien civilisation might use different concepts of time, space, identity or intelligence. A signal that appears simple could contain meanings humans are not prepared to recognise.

This challenge appears throughout [science fiction]({{ 'science-fiction/' | relative_url }}), but *Contact* focuses on the scientific version of the problem. Unlike stories where aliens immediately speak fluent human languages, Sagan’s story presents communication as slow, uncertain and dependent on careful reasoning. The message must be reconstructed step by step rather than instantly translated.

Real-world experiments continue to explore similar questions. The SETI Institute’s “A Sign in Space” project, for example, involved transmitting an artificial Mars-orbiting signal as an exercise in public decoding. ## Human reactions to contact

### Discovery becomes a global event

In *Contact*, the signal does not remain a scientific curiosity. Once confirmed, it becomes a political, religious and social event affecting humanity as a whole. The discovery challenges existing ideas about humanity’s place in the universe and forces governments, scientists and the public to respond to information unlike anything previously encountered.

This focus distinguishes *Contact* from many alien-invasion stories. The tension comes not from an immediate physical threat but from uncertainty. Humans must decide whether the message is a gift, a risk, a test or simply information from a civilisation with motives that cannot be understood.

Real SETI discussions similarly emphasise the importance of careful communication after a confirmed detection. Existing principles recommend verification through independent observations and responsible public disclosure before conclusions are announced. They also distinguish discovering a signal from deciding whether humanity should reply.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seti.org/research/seti-101/protocols-for-an-eti-signal-detection/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seti.org">[SETI Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seti.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Institute Protocols for an ETI Signal Detection</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SETI InstituteProtocols for an ETI Signal DetectionJune 1, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1, 2026</span></span></span>

### Why the signal matters more than the alien itself


<div class="content-callout content-callout--key content-callout--takeaway" markdown="1">

The lasting importance of *Contact* is that the first evidence of extraterrestrial intelligence is not a creature but a conversation attempt. The story turns a UFO and science-fiction tradition of encounters into a question about knowledge: how does one intelligence recognise another?

</div>

By focusing on a distant signal, Sagan shifts first contact away from spectacle and towards scientific interpretation. The mystery is not simply whether aliens exist, but whether humans can identify intelligence when it arrives in a form completely unlike anything they have experienced. This remains one of the most enduring ideas in science fiction about [extraterrestrial life]({{ 'extraterrestrial-life/' | relative_url }}) and one of the closest fictional reflections of the real challenges facing SETI research.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seti.org/news/contact-movie/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seti.org">[SETI Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seti.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Institute Contact (Movie</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SETI InstituteContact (Movie)August 22, 2011...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 22, 2011</span></span></span>

  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
